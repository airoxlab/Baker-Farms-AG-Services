# Baker Farms & AG Services LLC

Single-page sample website for Baker Farms & AG Services LLC, an Ohio farm with a farm shop and agricultural services.

- **Phone:** (937) 564-5733
- **Hours:** Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 8 PM; Sunday closed (from Google)
- **Address:** none published. The Google listing is a service-area profile with no street address.

## Structure

- `index.html`: the whole site. CSS is inline and there's no build step. The workwear/equipment identity uses Barlow Condensed and Barlow in charcoal, wheat gold and rust, with light and dark themes.
- `img/`: stock photos from Unsplash (baler at sunset, sprayer, square bales, aerial harvest, tractors, crop rows, corn). They're credited in the footer as representative, not the business's own.
